Your Solar Hub **Maintain** tab shows warranty terms calculated from your commissioning date and equipment on record. This guide explains what is typically covered and how to raise a claim.

## Standard coverage (typical)

| Component | Typical term | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Solar panels | Up to 25 years product | Brand-specific; see equipment card |
| Inverter | Often 5 years | Manufacturer warranty; extensions vary by brand |
| Workmanship | Rayenna installation | Defects in our installation work |

Exact terms depend on the brands on **your** project. Always check **Maintain → Warranty** for live dates.

## What is usually covered

- Manufacturing defects in panels or inverter
- Premature failure under normal operating conditions
- Installation defects attributable to Rayenna workmanship

## What is usually not covered

- Flood, fire, lightning, or vandalism
- Damage from unauthorized third-party modifications
- Shading, soiling, or grid outages affecting output
- Consumables and cosmetic wear
- Animal damage (may fall under home insurance)

## How to check your dates

1. Open **Maintain → Warranty**.
2. Each row shows total years, years remaining, and expiry date.
3. **Profile** also lists equipment specifications.

Contact Support if a component is missing or dates look wrong — we will correct CRM records.

## Making a warranty claim

1. Go to **Maintain → Report an issue**.
2. Subject: **Warranty claim**.
3. Describe the fault and note any **inverter error codes**.
4. Add photos if safe to do so.
5. Rayenna verifies coverage and coordinates with the manufacturer if needed.

**Safety:** If you smell burning, see smoke, or have exposed live wiring, call **Support → Emergency** immediately and keep people away from the equipment.

## Extending inverter warranty

Some inverter brands offer extended warranties at extra cost. Ask Support before expiry if you are interested — we can confirm eligibility for your model.
