Clean panels produce more energy. Rayenna includes a **scheduled cleaning plan** for your project so you do not need to climb the roof yourself.

## Your Rayenna cleaning schedule

Visits are anchored to your **net meter installation date** (recorded in CRM as the subsidy / net meter milestone):

- **First visit** — 6 months after net meter installation
- **Then** — every 6 months
- **Duration** — 5 years from that anchor (10 visits total)

Upcoming and completed visits appear under **Maintain → Maintenance schedule**.

## Why professional cleaning matters

- Roof access risks falls and electrical hazards
- Wrong detergents or high-pressure washing can damage panels or void warranty
- Technicians check for loose clamps, cable wear, and obvious damage during visits

## Between scheduled visits

- Rinse dust with plain water only if you can reach panels **safely from ground level** — never climb a wet roof
- Report new **bird droppings**, pollen buildup, or construction dust if generation drops sharply in **Track**
- Do not place objects on panels or walk on them unless you are a trained installer

## If a visit is overdue

Check **Maintain** for the due date label. If status shows overdue, use **Maintain → Schedule service** or **Support → Send a Query** with subject **Panel cleaning**.

## Wrong net meter date?

The entire schedule depends on the correct net meter installation date. If your meter was installed earlier or later than shown, contact Support so we can update CRM and regenerate your schedule.
