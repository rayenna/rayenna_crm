After your rooftop solar system is installed, **net metering** lets KSEB credit the units you export to the grid. Rayenna coordinates most of the paperwork, but it helps to know what happens at each stage.

## The five main steps

1. **Application** — Rayenna submits a net metering application to your KSEB section office with technical documents (SLD, commissioning report, capacity details).
2. **Feasibility / technical approval** — KSEB verifies your connection, sanctioned load, and system size.
3. **Net meter installation** — A bi-directional meter is installed at your premises (often replacing or alongside your existing meter).
4. **Testing & synchronization** — The meter is tested and linked to your consumer number in KSEB billing systems.
5. **Solar billing activation** — Your bill starts showing import, export, and net units. Credits apply per applicable tariff rules.

Timelines vary by section office and workload. Commissioning can finish before billing switches — that is normal.

## What you may be asked to provide

- Recent electricity bill with **consumer number**
- Identity proof and property ownership / tax receipt
- Signed KSEB application forms (Rayenna will guide you)
- Access for meter installation on scheduled date

## How to check status

Use **Support → Send a Query** with subject **Net meter status**. Include your site address and consumer number if you have it. We match your query to the CRM project and reply with the latest DISCOM stage.

## After the net meter is live

- Generation in **Track** and KSEB billing may not align on day one — allow one or two billing cycles.
- Keep a copy of your last **pre-solar** bill for comparison.
- Panel cleaning visits in **Maintain** are scheduled from your **net meter installation date** (six months later, then every six months for five years).
