# Privacy and account

Practices that apply to **every role** — Sales, Operations, Finance, Management, and Admin.

## Login and sessions

- Sign in with your **email** and **password** on the Rayenna CRM login page.
- Traffic between your browser and the server uses **HTTPS** (encrypted in transit).
- The system **logs you out after a period of inactivity** for security. Log in again to continue; change your password if you are concerned about unauthorised access ([FAQ](/help/faq)).
- On **shared or public computers**, use **Logout** from the user menu when you finish.

## Your password

- Minimum length is **6 characters** (enforced on create and change).
- Passwords are stored securely on the server — not shown in plain text in the app.
- **Change your own password** anytime from the user menu → **Change Password** ([Change password guide](#change-password)).
- If you **forget** your password, you cannot self-reset from **Change Password** (it requires the current password). An **Administrator** generates a **one-time reset link** from [Users](#users) (valid **24 hours**).

## Role-based access

- You only see **menus and actions** allowed for your role.
- **Project and customer data** may be **scoped** (for example, Sales sees own assigned projects; Finance sees payment fields on all projects). Full rules: [Roles → Permission matrix](/help/roles#permission-matrix).
- Opening a URL for a page your role cannot use shows **Access denied** — that is expected.

## Data you work with

- **Customer** names, contacts, addresses, and project records are business-confidential. Use them only for Rayenna work.
- **Documents** uploaded to projects (photos, PDFs, sheets) follow project permissions — see [Key Artifacts](/help/modules#key-artifacts-module).
- **My Day** (tasks, journal, reminders) is **private to your account** and syncs across devices after login — not shared with teammates ([Zenith → My Day](/help/zenith#my-day-personal-productivity-drawer)).

## Account hygiene

| Do | Avoid |
| :-- | :-- |
| Use a **unique** password for this CRM | Reusing passwords from personal sites |
| **Log out** on shared PCs | Leaving sessions open on kiosk machines |
| Report suspicious logins or data issues to **Admin** | Sharing your password or reset links in chat groups |
| Keep your **browser updated** | Ignoring repeated failed-login alerts (Admins should investigate) |

## What Administrators can see

- **Audit & Security** logs sign-in attempts, sensitive actions, and exports ([Audit and Security](#audit-and-security)).
- Admins manage accounts on **Users** — they do not use **Change Password** on your behalf; they send **reset links** when needed.

For admin procedures, see [Users](#users) and [Administrator role guide](/help/roles#administrator-role-guide).
