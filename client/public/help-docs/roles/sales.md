# Sales Role Guide

You own the **pipeline**: customers, leads, proposals, and projects assigned to you. This guide covers daily work, permissions, and where to go deeper.

## At a glance

| You can | You cannot |
| :-- | :-- |
| View and edit **your** projects (assigned salesperson) | Open or edit another salesperson’s projects |
| Create **customers** and **projects** | Delete projects or export full project lists |
| Full **customer** edit when you are the assigned salesperson | Access **Users**, **Audit & Security**, or **Tally export** |
| **Support tickets** on your projects | Add follow-ups on **closed** tickets (Admin only) |
| **Sales** dashboard and **Zenith** (sales layout) | Change another user’s salesperson assignment |

→ [Permission matrix](#permission-matrix) · [Modules → Projects](/help/modules#projects-module)

## Your dashboard

Open **Dashboard** from the top menu (or **Zenith ✦** for the command center).

**Typical metrics:** leads, pipeline value, capacity, revenue, site survey / proposal counts, at-risk deals.

**Filters:** financial year and month narrow every card and chart.

**Proposal Engine:** on the classic dashboard, the **Proposal Engine** card and Zenith **Your focus** buckets appear for Sales when projects are in proposal-related stages. [Modules](/help/modules) · [Zenith](/help/zenith#zenith-command-center)

**Drill-down:** many charts and KPI tiles open **Projects** or a quick drawer with the same filters.

## Customers

1. **Customers** → **+ New Customer** (or **Ctrl+Shift+N** / **⌘⇧N**).
2. Enter name, **customer type** (Residential, Apartment, Commercial), and at least one phone number.
3. Save, then link projects to that customer.

**Editing:** you may edit a customer when you are the **currently assigned salesperson**. Management and Admin can always edit and reassign.

**Viewing:** you see all customers in the list; use filters to focus on your accounts.

Details: [Modules → Customer Master](/help/modules#customer-master-module)

## Projects and leads

### Create a project

1. **Projects** → **+ New Project** (or **Ctrl+Shift+E** / **⌘⇧E**).
2. Select customer, **segment** (subsidy / non-subsidy / commercial), service type, capacity (whole kW), value, **lead source** (+ required details for Referral, Channel Partner, Other).
3. Set status **Lead** for new opportunities; save.

### Work your pipeline

- Filter by **status** (Lead → Proposal → Confirmed).
- Open a row → **Edit** to update commercial fields, financing, status, documents.
- **Deal health** and **lead source** pills in the table — hover or tap for detail.

You only see projects where **you** are the salesperson. If a colleague’s project does not appear, that is expected.

Turn on **Needs review** to list deals whose dates, payments, or system size do not match stage. On **Zenith**, the same flags appear in **Things needing attention**. Saving with commissioning before confirmation, or advance above order value, asks you to confirm. Guide: [Needs review](/help/modules#needs-review-data-sense).

### Proposals

For projects in **Proposal** or **Confirmed**, use **Proposals (New)** / Proposal Engine from the project or dashboard card. [Modules](/help/modules)

## Support tickets

1. Open a project → **Support tickets** (or **Support Tickets** module).
2. Create a ticket, add follow-ups, close when resolved.

Finance users do not use this module. You cannot add notes to tickets already **closed** unless you are Admin.

## Payments and finance fields

You can **view** payment status and balances on your projects. **Entering payments** is for **Finance** and **Admin** only.

## Exports

**Projects Excel/CSV** and **customer bulk export** are **Admin only**. Ask your administrator if you need a data extract.

## Daily rhythm

1. Check **Dashboard** or **Zenith** for pipeline, **Things needing attention**, and at-risk deals.
2. Update **lead / survey / proposal** stages on active projects; clear **Needs review** flags when the record is wrong.
3. Create customers and projects for new opportunities.
4. Log **support tickets** when customers need follow-up.
5. Use **My Day** (**Ctrl+Shift+M** / **⌘⇧M**) to pin follow-ups from the dashboard or Hit List.
