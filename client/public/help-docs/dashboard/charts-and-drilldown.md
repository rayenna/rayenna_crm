## Classic Dashboard — chart click-through to Projects

On the **classic Dashboard** (not Zenith), most charts are **clickable**. Clicking a **bar**, **FY column** (revenue vs profit separately), **pie slice**, **word** in the profitability cloud, or **Top 10** row opens **Projects** with filters matching the slice, plus dashboard **FY / Quarter / Month** where applicable.

- Tooltips often say **Click to open Projects →** (or **Click slice** / **Click bar**).
- **Semantics** align with Zenith where the same slice exists — Dashboard goes **direct to Projects**; Zenith usually opens **Quick Actions** first ([Zenith → Explore](/help/zenith#explore-charts-and-drill-down-zenith)).
- **Customer profitability** — word or **Top 10** row opens **Projects** with **search** (customer name) and revenue analytics slice. Zenith: [Customer projects profitability](/help/zenith#customer-projects-profitability-zenith).

If your role does not see a chart, that click path is not available.
