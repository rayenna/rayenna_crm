# Customers, projects, and data

## Customers

### How do I create a customer?
**Customers** → **New Customer**, or **Ctrl+Shift+N** / **⌘⇧N**, or `/customers?new=1` when your role allows. Choose **Customer type** (Residential, Apartment, Commercial) and complete the matching fields. **Sales**, **Management**, and **Admin** can create customers. Guide: [Customer Master](/help/modules#customer-master-module).

### Who can edit or delete customers?
**Admin** and **Management** edit any customer. **Sales** edits customers **assigned to them**. **Admin** deletes customers with **no projects**. **Management** can reassign salesperson but not delete. [Customer Master](/help/modules#customer-master-module) · [Permission matrix](/help/roles#permission-matrix).

## Projects

### How do I create a new project?
**Projects** → **New Project** (**Ctrl+Shift+E** / **⌘⇧E** when allowed). Select a **customer** first (create in Customer Master if needed). Fill segment, type, capacity, value, lead source, etc. **Sales** and **Admin** create projects. Guide: [Projects module](/help/modules#projects-module).

### How do I update project status?
Open **Project detail** → **Edit** (if your role allows). Change **status** and related fields in the form, then save. Status gates what Operations and Finance can see — [Projects module](/help/modules#projects-module).

### Can I upload documents to projects?
Yes. **Project detail** → **Key Artifacts** — upload photos, PDFs, and sheets. Guide: [Key Artifacts](/help/modules#key-artifacts-module).

### Why don't dashboard and project filters match?
Dashboard **FY / Quarter / Month** apply to tiles and charts. Opening **Projects** from a Quick Access tile **passes** those dates; filters you set manually on Projects stay until you **Clear All** or click a dashboard tile again. [Dashboard → Filters](/help/dashboard#dashboard-filters).

## Support Tickets

### How do I create a support ticket?
From **Project detail** → **Support / Service Tickets** → **Create Ticket** (not available for **Lost** projects). Or **Support Tickets** in the top menu (**Ctrl+Shift+K** / **⌘⇧K**). Guide: [Support Tickets](/help/modules#support-tickets-module).

### How do I add follow-ups to tickets?
Open the ticket (list or project) → **Ticket Details** drawer → add a follow-up. Timeline shows history. [Support Tickets](/help/modules#support-tickets-module).

### Can I close tickets?
**Sales**, **Operations**, **Management**, and **Admin** can close tickets on projects they can access. After close, only **Admin** adds follow-ups on closed tickets. **Finance** has no Support Tickets menu. [Support Tickets](/help/modules#support-tickets-module).

## Data and reports

### How do I export data?
Use export actions where your role allows — **Projects** and **Customers** exports are **Admin** only; **Tally Export** for Finance handoff. [Permission matrix](/help/roles#permission-matrix).

### Where is Tally Export and who can use it?
Top menu → **Tally Export** (**Finance** and **Admin**). Export Projects, Invoices, or Payments as Excel, CSV, or Tally XML with optional date filters. Guide: [Tally Export](/help/modules#tally-export-module).

### Can I filter data?
Yes — **status**, FY/Quarter/Month on Dashboard and Projects; **My / All customers** on Customers; role-specific project filters. Module guides: [Modules](/help/modules).

### How do I search?
Use each module’s **search** bar — customer name, ID, or consumer number on **Customers**; customer or project fields on **Projects**.
