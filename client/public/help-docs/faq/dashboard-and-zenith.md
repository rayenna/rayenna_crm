# Dashboard and Zenith

### How do I navigate between modules?
Use the **top navigation menu**. Options depend on your role. Module guides: [Modules](/help/modules).

### What keyboard shortcuts are available?
Press **?** (outside text fields) for context-sensitive Help. **Ctrl+Shift+** + letter (**⌘⇧** on Mac): **D** Dashboard, **C** Customers, **P** Projects, **K** Support Tickets, **Z** Zenith, **N** new customer, **E** new project — when your role allows. **Ctrl+Shift+M** opens **My Day**. **Esc** on Help returns to Dashboard. Full table: [Getting Started → Keyboard shortcuts](/help/getting-started#keyboard-shortcuts).

### What is the Dashboard?
The **classic Dashboard** is your in-menu analytics home: FY filters, role-specific KPIs, **Quick Access** tiles, and charts. Layout differs by role. Overview: [Dashboard help](/help/dashboard).

### What is Zenith?
**Zenith ✦** is the full-screen **command center** (Dashboard menu → **Zenith ✦**). Same **FY / Quarter / Month** rules as the Dashboard, plus Hit List, AI Insights, **Quick Actions** drawers, and optional offline PWA. Overview: [Zenith command center](/help/zenith#zenith-command-center).

### What are the Dashboard filters (FY, Quarter, Month)?
**Financial Year** is always available; **Quarter** and **Month** unlock when **exactly one** FY is selected. Tiles and most charts respect these filters. Details: [Dashboard → Filters](/help/dashboard#dashboard-filters) · [Year-on-Year KPIs](/help/dashboard#year-on-year-kpis).

### What are Quick Access tiles on the Dashboard?
Metric tiles and list cards (e.g. leads, **Payment Status**, **Proposal Engine**) show counts for your filters. Clicking opens **Projects** with FY/Quarter/Month plus the tile’s slice applied. Guide: [Dashboard → Quick Access](/help/dashboard#quick-access-tiles).

### Can I open Projects from Dashboard charts?
**Classic Dashboard** — most charts click through **directly to Projects** with matching filters. **Zenith** — most explore charts open **Quick Actions** first; use **Open in Projects →** for the full grid. **Customer profitability** in both can jump straight to Projects. Details: [Dashboard → Chart click-through](/help/dashboard#classic-dashboard-chart-click-through-to-projects) · [Zenith → Explore charts](/help/zenith#explore-charts-and-drill-down-zenith).

### What is “Things needing attention” on the Dashboard?
**Sales**, **Operations**, and **Admin** may see a card beside **Today's plan** when late-stage projects lack **panel and/or inverter brand**. Rows include **+ My Day** and **Open →**; **Projects →** uses **Lifecycle specs incomplete**. **Management** and **Finance** do not see the card. Zenith **daily briefing** lifecycle line: **Sales**, **Admin**, and **Operations** only. [Dashboard → Things needing attention](/help/dashboard#things-needing-attention-dashboard) · [Zenith → Daily briefing](/help/zenith#daily-briefing-zenith).
