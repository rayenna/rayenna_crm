# Finance playbook — payment radar

A focused flow for **Finance** users monitoring collections and loan exposure in Zenith.

## 1. Open Zenith with the right period (~2 min)

1. **Dashboard** menu → **Zenith ✦**.
2. Set **FY** (single year unlocks **Quarter** and **Month**). Align with your reconciliation calendar — numbers match [Dashboard filters](/help/dashboard#dashboard-filters).
3. Confirm **Live** status before logging payments or opening drawers that write data.

## 2. KPI strip — cash and loans (~3 min)

1. On the **KPI strip**, review **revenue received**, **outstanding**, and related finance tiles ([KPI strip](#kpi-strip-and-year-on-year)).
2. Check the **Weighted open pipeline** tile for concentration and the **open balance** accent ([Weighted open pipeline](#weighted-open-pipeline-revenue-forecast-tile)).
2. Click **Availing Loan** (when shown) — opens a **Quick Actions** list of projects in loan-related states ([Explore shortcuts](#charts-funnels-and-shortcuts)).
3. Note YoY badges — use them to spot collection or recognition shifts vs prior year.

**Phone:** **KPIs** tab first, then switch to **Payments** ([Mobile navigation](#mobile-navigation-and-layout-zenith)).

## 3. Payment radar in Your Focus (~8 min)

1. Open the **Payments** mobile tab or scroll to **Your Focus** → **Payment radar** ([Payment radar](#payment-radar-finance)).
2. **Top overdue** — work oldest / largest balances first; row opens finance **Quick Actions** with **Payment** snapshot (**status**, **total received**, **balance pending**).
3. **Latest payments received** — verify recent postings against bank credits.
4. Use the shared **payment status** legend so pill colours match **Project detail** and dashboard **Payment status** card.
5. From a drawer, **Open in Projects →** exports or bulk review with the same slice.

## 4. Funnel and charts (~5 min)

1. **Deal flow funnel** — click **payment** pills (e.g. overdue, partial) for filtered lists ([Deal flow funnel](#deal-flow-funnel-zenith)).
2. **Explore the landscape** — revenue / profit by FY, customer profitability word cloud or **Top 10** ([Customer projects profitability](#customer-projects-profitability-zenith)).
3. **Revenue and profit by FY** chart — click a FY point for drawer drill-down ([Revenue and profit by FY chart](#revenue-and-profit-by-fy-chart-zenith)).

Finance Zenith does **not** show the Sales **lifecycle briefing** line for missing panel/inverter brands.

## 5. Handoff to Projects or Tally

- Update payment fields on **Project detail** when drawer review finds gaps ([Modules → Projects](/help/modules#projects-module)).
- Use **Tally Export** when period close requires ledger extract ([Tally Export](/help/modules#tally-export-module)).

## Checklist

- [ ] Overdue top rows reviewed or assigned
- [ ] Latest receipts spot-checked
- [ ] Loan concentration KPI understood for the period
- [ ] Drawer filters match the question you asked (FY / month)
