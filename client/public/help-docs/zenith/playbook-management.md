# Management playbook — executive review

A **weekly or monthly** executive pass through Zenith for **Management** (and **Admin** analytics layout). Company-wide numbers — not limited to one salesperson.

## 1. Frame the period (~2 min)

1. **Dashboard** menu → **Zenith ✦**.
2. Select **one FY** to unlock **Quarter** and **Month** for board-ready slices.
3. Open **✦ Briefing** if you skipped the auto dialog — note **Your My Day** only if you use personal tasks; **Management** does **not** see the Sales/Ops **lifecycle brand** briefing line ([Daily briefing](#daily-briefing-zenith)).

## 2. Executive KPIs and forecast (~5 min)

1. Scan the **KPI strip** with YoY badges and sparklines ([KPI strip](#kpi-strip-and-year-on-year)).
2. Read the wide **Revenue Forecast** tile when shown ([Revenue Forecast](#revenue-forecast-wide-kpi-tile)).
3. Click **AI Insights** lines that point to revenue, pipeline, or concentration risks ([AI Insights](#ai-insights-ticker)).

**Phone:** **Today** tab — **Hit List** plus KPIs ([Executive layout](#executive-sales-management-admin)).

## 3. Hit List and pipeline (~5 min)

1. **Today’s Hit List** — seven urgent deals across the company; sort by **Alert** or deal value ([Layout stability and Hit List](#layout-stability-and-hit-list-zenith)).
2. Expand **Your Focus** executive buckets — **Your pipeline today** by stage or owner.
3. Drill via **Quick Actions**; avoid editing in drawer — use **Open in Projects →** only when delegating follow-up with full context.

## 4. The Board and funnel (~5 min)

1. **The Board** — salesperson ranking for the filtered period ([The Board](#the-board-leaderboard)).
2. **Deal flow funnel** — stage mix and conversion; click stages for underlying lists ([Deal flow funnel](#deal-flow-funnel-zenith)).
3. Celebrate **Victory toast** wins with the team when you are live in Zenith ([Victory toast](#victory-toast-stage-wins)).

## 5. Landscape charts (~8 min)

1. **Revenue and profit by FY** — trend vs prior years ([Revenue and profit by FY chart](#revenue-and-profit-by-fy-chart-zenith)).
2. **Customer projects profitability** — word cloud or **Top 10** customers ([Customer projects profitability](#customer-projects-profitability-zenith)).
3. **Explore** treemap / segment charts — click through to verify outliers ([Explore charts and drill-down](#explore-charts-and-drill-down-zenith)).
4. **Panel and inverter brand** bars — supply mix and lifecycle completeness ([Panel and inverter brand charts](#panel-and-inverter-brand-charts-zenith)).

## 6. Compare with classic Dashboard

**Today's plan** and **Things needing attention** appear on Zenith for Management (Needs review plus, for Admin, lifecycle brand gaps). Use **by salesperson** on the attention card, then **Open in Projects →** to coach without editing.

## Executive checklist

- [ ] Period filters documented for the review meeting
- [ ] **Needs review** rollup checked (salesperson counts) and outliers assigned
- [ ] Board ranking discussed with sales lead
- [ ] Profitability and FY chart surprises investigated via drawer → Projects
- [ ] Actions assigned in Projects / remarks, not only in Zenith
