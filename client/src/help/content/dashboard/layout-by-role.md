# Layout by role

Quick Access **tile order** and surrounding KPIs depend on your role. **Payment Status** appears for Sales, Operations, Finance, and Management. **Proposal Engine** appears for Sales, Management, and Admin.

## Sales

**Wide screen** (three tiles per row):

1. **Row 1:** My Leads · Site Survey Stage · Proposal Stage  
2. **Row 2:** Open Deals · My Confirmed Orders · Under Installation  
3. **Row 3:** **Payment Status** (list) · Completed Installation · **Proposal Engine** (list)

**Open Deals** = Lead + Site Survey + Proposal (same as legacy “My Open Deals”).

**Phone:** tiles stack in that order. **Tablet / narrow:** usually two per row; row 3 list cards often **full width** each for readability.

**Wide row 3:** Payment Status and Proposal Engine **match height**; Completed Installation stays compact and centred between them.

**Data scope:** metrics and charts are **your projects only** (assigned salesperson).

---

## Management and Admin

**Row 1:** Total Leads · Site Survey Stage · Proposal Stage · Open Deals  

**Row 2:** Confirmed Orders · Under Installation · Completed Installation · Subsidy Credited  

**Row 3:** **Payment Status** · **Availing Loan** · **Proposal Engine**

Wide bottom row: Payment Status and Proposal Engine **same height**; Availing Loan stays compact in the middle.

**Admin** uses this same classic Dashboard layout as Management. **Things needing attention** appears on **Zenith**, not here.

---

## Operations

**One band of four tiles:** Pending Installation · Completed Installation · Subsidy Credited · **Payment Status**

No Availing Loan tile. No Proposal Engine card.

### Pending Subsidy list

When projects are waiting on subsidy credit, an extra card may appear below Quick Access:

- Title: **Pending Subsidy (N)**  
- Each row: **customer name** and **days pending**  
- Informational list (not a Quick Access drill-down tile) — open **Projects** or project detail to update subsidy dates in **lifecycle**.

**Company-wide** data (not salesperson-scoped).

---

## Finance

**Top KPI row** (above Quick Access): **Total Revenue**, **Amount Received**, **Outstanding Balance**.

**Quick Access band** (wraps on narrow screens): Pending Installation · Completed Installation · Subsidy Credited · **Availing Loan** · **Payment Status**

No Proposal Engine. No YoY band on classic Dashboard.

## Role-based content (charts & sections)

| Role | Beyond Quick Access |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Sales** | [YoY KPIs](#year-on-year-kpis), stage & FY charts, lead source, customer type pies, profitability cloud, loans by bank, panel/inverter brand bars |
| **Operations** | Pending Subsidy (when data exists), stage chart, revenue by sales team, FY chart, customer type pie, panel/inverter bars |
| **Finance** | Revenue KPIs, lead source, sales team, FY chart, customer type pie, profitability cloud, loans by bank |
| **Management / Admin** | YoY KPIs, executive tiles, treemap, full chart grid, panel/inverter bars |

Chart details: [Charts and visualizations](#charts-and-visualizations).

Empty list rows are **omitted** — cards only show buckets that exist in your data.
