# Change password

Update **your own** account password. Every logged-in role can use this page; you cannot change another user’s password here.

## Who can access

- **All logged-in users** — open **Change Password** from the **user menu** (top right, your name).
- To reset **another** user’s password, Administrators use **Reset password** on the [Users](#users) page.

## Open the page

1. Click your **name** (top right) → **Change Password**.
2. The page shows **Change Password**, your **name and email**, and fields for **Current Password**, **New Password**, and **Confirm New Password**.

## Change your password

1. Enter **Current Password** (required). Use the **eye** icon to show or hide the field.
2. Enter **New Password** (required) — at least **6 characters**. Prefer a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
3. Enter the same value in **Confirm New Password**. If they do not match, the form shows an error.
4. Click **Change Password**. On success, you see a confirmation and return to the **Dashboard**. You **stay logged in** with the new password.
5. Click **Cancel** to return to the Dashboard without saving.

If the **current password is wrong**, the change is rejected. If you **forgot** the current password, ask an Administrator for a **Reset password** link from [Users](#users).

## After you change it

- Use the new password on your next login (other devices or after logout).
- Do not share the new password.
- On shared computers, **log out** when finished.

## Best practices

- Do not reuse passwords from other systems.
- Change your password after any suspected compromise or per company policy.
- Administrators: prefer sending a **reset link** so the user sets their own password rather than sharing a temporary password in plain text.
