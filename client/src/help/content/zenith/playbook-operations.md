# Operations playbook — installation pulse

How **Operations** users use Zenith for execution visibility, lifecycle quality, and brand analytics.

## 1. Land in Zenith (~2 min)

1. **Dashboard** menu → **Zenith ✦**.
2. Set **FY** / **Quarter** / **Month** to match your installation reporting window.
3. Read the **daily briefing** if shown — **Sales**, **Admin**, and **Operations** may see a **panel/inverter brand** reminder for late-stage jobs missing specs ([Daily briefing](#daily-briefing-zenith)). Cross-check [Things needing attention on Zenith](/help/dashboard#things-needing-attention-dashboard).

## 2. KPIs and Ops tab (~4 min)

1. Review execution-oriented **KPI strip** tiles ([KPI strip](#kpi-strip-and-year-on-year)).
2. Scan **Weighted open pipeline** for Confirmed+ value / kW in the role accent ([Weighted open pipeline](#weighted-open-pipeline-revenue-forecast-tile)).
2. On mobile, use **KPIs** then **Ops** tabs ([Mobile navigation](#mobile-navigation-and-layout-zenith)).
3. Open **Your Focus** → **Installation pulse** — tables of jobs under installation, subsidy pending, or similar ops cohorts ([Installation pulse](#installation-pulse-operations)).

## 3. Work the queue (~8 min)

For each hot project:

1. Row click → **Quick Actions** (single-project or list mode).
2. Read **Recent remarks** and **Payment** snapshot when payment applies (**N/A** otherwise) — same pattern as Sales drawers.
3. **Log activity** for site visits, vendor delays, or customer coordination.
4. **Open in Projects →** to update **status**, **panel/inverter brand**, documents, or subsidy fields.

Use **Deal Health** badges in tables to prioritise at-risk installs ([Deal Health Score](#deal-health-score-zenith)).

## 4. Funnel and Operations band (~3 min)

1. **Deal flow funnel** — click **Under Installation**, **Completed**, or subsidy-related stages ([Deal flow funnel](#deal-flow-funnel-zenith)).
2. Scroll the **Operations (Zenith)** section when present for role-specific summary cards ([Operations](#operations-zenith)).

## 5. Brand charts (~3 min)

1. Open the **Charts** tab on mobile or scroll to **Explore the landscape**.
2. **Projects by panel brand** and **Projects by inverter brand** — hover for counts, capacity sum, and cost hints; click for **Quick Actions** ([Panel and inverter brand charts](#panel-and-inverter-brand-charts-zenith)).
3. Fix missing brands in **Projects** so future analytics cohorts stay complete.

## Checklist

- [ ] Installation pulse rows triaged
- [ ] Lifecycle briefing count trending down
- [ ] Brand gaps closed on active sites
- [ ] Activity logged for customer-visible delays
