# Sales playbook — morning in Zenith

A practical **15–20 minute** flow for Sales users. Adjust filters to your current FY focus.

## 1. Open and set context (~2 min)

1. Top menu → **Dashboard** menu → **Zenith ✦** (or **Ctrl+Shift+Z** / **⌘⇧Z** from anywhere when not typing).
2. In the **command bar**, set **Financial Year** (and **Quarter** / **Month** if you are reviewing a specific period). Use **Reset** if numbers look unexpectedly narrow.
3. Glance at **Live** vs **Offline** — My Day and fresh CRM writes need **Live**; cached KPIs may still show offline on PWA.
4. If the **Smart daily briefing** appears, scan **Your My Day**, any **Needs review** line, and any **lifecycle brand** reminder ([Daily briefing](#daily-briefing-zenith) · [Needs review](/help/modules#needs-review-data-sense)). Dismiss or **Don’t show again today** as you prefer.

## 2. Scan ribbons, Ask, and KPIs (~3 min)

1. Skim **Solar News** — optional industry context; click a headline to read externally.
2. Try **Ask Zenith** for a quick question (e.g. pipeline by stage or support tickets) or a task jump (**New Customer**, **Open My Day**) — same tasks as **Ctrl+K** / **⌘K** ([Ask Zenith](#ask-zenith-natural-language)).
3. On the **KPI strip**, note pipeline value, capacity, and any red trend badges ([KPI strip](#kpi-strip-and-year-on-year)).
4. Read **AI Insights** under the tiles — click a line to jump to **The Board**, funnel, or **Your Focus** ([AI Insights](#ai-insights-ticker)).
5. Optionally expand **The Board** (starts collapsed) for this period’s ranking ([The Board](#the-board-leaderboard)).

**Phone:** Zenith opens on the **Today** tab (Solar News, KPIs, AI Insights, Board, Hit List). Other tabs hide the news/insights tickers ([Mobile navigation](#mobile-navigation-and-layout-zenith)).

## 3. Work the Hit List (~5 min)

1. Open **Today’s Hit List** (up to seven urgent deals).
2. Use **filters** and **sort** like **Your pipeline today** — focus on **Alert** rows and stale confirmations first ([Layout stability and Hit List](#layout-stability-and-hit-list-zenith)).
3. For each priority deal:
   - **Open →** or row click → **Quick Actions** drawer.
   - Read **Recent remarks** before **Log activity**.
   - Pin a follow-up with **+ My Day** if you will call later ([My Day](#my-day-personal-productivity-drawer)).
4. When you need the full project form, use **Open in Projects →** in the drawer footer.

## 4. Your Focus and Proposal Engine (~5 min)

1. Expand **Your Focus** panels you use daily — collapse the rest to reduce scroll.
2. Review **Your pipeline today** buckets; open weak **Deal Health** scores ([Deal Health Score](#deal-health-score-zenith)).
3. Check **Proposal Engine** rows for projects in proposal stages — same drawer pattern ([Sales pipeline & PE](#sales-pipeline-activity-and-proposal-engine)).
4. If the **Support tickets** strip shows overdue or Solar Hub counts, open it and work those customers the same morning.
5. Optional: open **☀ My Day** (**Ctrl+Shift+M**) and tick off pinned tasks; leave **Log to project remarks when done** on if the team should see completion.

## 5. Funnel and competitive pulse (~3 min)

1. Open the **Deal flow funnel** (Pipeline tab on phone) — click a stage to open the matching **Quick Actions** list ([Deal flow funnel](#deal-flow-funnel-zenith)).
2. If you skipped it earlier, expand **The Board** on Today for ranking ([The Board](#the-board-leaderboard)).
3. Watch for a **Victory toast** when a deal hits a winning stage ([Victory toast](#victory-toast-stage-wins)).

## 6. Before you leave Zenith

- [ ] Hit List top items touched or scheduled in **My Day**
- [ ] **Things needing attention** scanned if the card is showing ([Needs review](/help/modules#needs-review-data-sense))
- [ ] Logged activity on deals you contacted
- [ ] PE / proposal stages reviewed
- [ ] Filters left sensible for next visit (or **Reset** for company-wide view)
