# Management Role Guide

You get a **company-wide lens**: every dashboard, **Zenith**, and read-only visibility into projects — plus targeted **write** access where leadership must act (customers, tickets, salesperson assignment).

## At a glance

| You can | You cannot |
| :-- | :-- |
| View **all** projects and customers | Edit project commercial, lifecycle, or payment fields |
| **Management** dashboard and **Zenith** (executive layout) | Create projects |
| **Create and edit any customer**; **reassign salesperson** | Delete projects or customers |
| **Support tickets** — create, follow up, close | **Tally export** (Finance / Admin) |
| **Proposal Engine** visibility on dashboard / Zenith | **Users** or **Audit & Security** (Admin) |
| **My Day** and full Help | Bulk Excel/CSV export (Admin) |

→ [Permission matrix](#permission-matrix) · [Zenith](/help/zenith#zenith-command-center)

## Your dashboard

**Typical metrics:** company leads, capacity, revenue, pipeline, profit, pending install / subsidy counts.

**Charts:** revenue by team, lead source, customer type, stage funnels — use FY/month filters for board-ready views.

**Zenith:** executive **Your focus**, Hit List, and landscape charts. [Zenith command center](/help/zenith#zenith-command-center)

**Proposal Engine card:** visible for Management (with Sales and Admin) when monitoring proposal-stage work.

## Projects (read-only)

- **Projects** lists **every** status including early pipeline and Lost.  
- Open any project for detail, documents, remarks, and payment **view**.  
- **Edit** is not available for project fields — escalate changes to **Sales** (own projects), **Operations** (execution), or **Finance** (payments).

Use filters (salesperson, status, PE buckets) to review team performance without changing data.

## Customers (limited write)

Management is a **governance** role on Customer Master:

1. **Create** customers when leadership captures a lead directly.  
2. **Edit** any customer record.  
3. Change **salesperson** assignment when accounts move between reps.

Shortcut: **Ctrl+Shift+N** / **⌘⇧N** for new customer when allowed.

Sales still runs day-to-day edits on accounts assigned to them.

## Support tickets

You have the same ticket **create / follow-up / close** rights as Sales and Operations — useful for escalations without editing the underlying project.

You **cannot** add follow-ups to **closed** tickets (Admin only).

## Finance and Tally

View payment status and financial KPIs on dashboards. **Tally export** remains Finance/Admin — ask Finance for accounting files.

## Analytics workflow

1. Start **Zenith** or **Dashboard** with the right FY/month.  
2. Drill from charts into **Projects** lists.  
3. Open detail for qualitative context (remarks, documents).  
4. Assign or correct **customers** when ownership is wrong.  
5. Open **support tickets** when a customer issue needs tracking without a project edit.

## Daily rhythm

1. Scan executive KPIs and **at-risk** / overdue signals.  
2. Review pipeline by **salesperson** or **lead source**.  
3. Reassign **customers** when team structure changes.  
4. Use **My Day** for leadership follow-ups (**Ctrl+Shift+M** / **⌘⇧M**).  
5. Pair with [Training guide](/help/training) when onboarding regional heads.
